1、[Archimedes' Series](https://www.futilitycloset.com/2025/11/17/all-the-way-down-2/)

In 200 BC, the ancient Greek mathematician Archimedes discovered that the sum of the infinite series 1/4 + 1/16 + 1/64 + 1/256 + ... is 1/3.

This can be seen from the diagram.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202512/bg2025120407.webp)

The figure above is a unit square. The largest black square has an area of 1/4, the second largest black square has an area of 1/16, and so on.

The black, white, and gray regions each occupy equal area in the overall figure, so the total area of all black squares must be 1/3.

There is another diagram.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202512/bg2025120408.webp)

In the figure above, if the largest triangle has an area of 1, then the largest black triangle has an area of 1/4, the second largest black triangle has an area of 1/16, and so on. The black, white, and gray regions each occupy equal proportions of the figure, so the total area of the black region must be 1/3.
