## RedSkill Promo

The most surprising thing this week was discovering that Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) is building the REDSkill community!

In my impression, Xiaohongshu belongs to the lifestyle and fashion community space, but the new REDSkill feature seems more designed for programmers.

Can you believe it? When posting notes on Xiaohongshu now, you can actually upload Skill files (Figure 1).

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202606/bg2026063017.webp)

Specifically, this feature is part of Xiaohongshu's AI assistant service. Your skill can be uploaded to Xiaohongshu, and other people's skills can be copied and shared to your AI Agent with one click.

Any Skill can be uploaded. Then, your note will have an additional "Red Skill" button — for example, a Skill for reducing Token consumption (Figure 2). http://xhslink.com/o/3SyhzanTPMH

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202606/bg2026063018.webp)

Users can click to view the Skill details, then copy the download URL (Figure 3) and paste it into their own Agent to install.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202607/bg2026070101.webp)

This means if you want to publish a Skill, you can use Xiaohongshu for hosting and distribution.

It combines a social media platform with a Skill hub. There's probably no precedent for this anywhere in the world. I saw in the comments that it wants to become the GitHub of Skills.

Xiaohongshu's goal might be to increase technical content on the platform, laying the foundation for the community's next step in AI transformation.

But for programmers, this is a great opportunity to expand your user base. Package your software service as a Skill, and you can reach Xiaohongshu's massive user base — something you can't achieve on other platforms. #rednote #skill #redskill
