## [Event] XEngineer Summer Training Camp

College students, listen up! Wondering how to develop your skills in the AI era without drowning in a sea of resumes and exams? Consider this summer's [XEngineer Training Camp](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/Ues5CUilqWqCgWMf3SZAAw?from=singlemessage&scene=1&subscene=93&sessionid=1780549607&clicktime=1780550030&enterid=1780550030&ascene=1&fasttmpl_type=0&fasttmpl_fullversion=8285001-zh_CN-zip&fasttmpl_flag=0&realreporttime=1780550030045).

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202606/bg2026060404.webp)

It was initiated by **Qi Niuyun (Qiniu) founder and CEO Xu Shiwei**, a listed company. It's open to college graduates and current students from the classes of 2025–2029, with no degree or major restrictions. **Just submit a project proposal or outcome, and you can apply.**

It trains participants in product and architecture skills for the AI era, guiding you to start from real needs, think things through, and then personally design, implement, and launch a project.

You'll experience real internet company workflows, develop practical skills, and gain job-seeking credentials and offer opportunities.

Visit **hr.qiniu.com** or scan the QR code below to register. The earlier you register, the earlier your spot is secured. The camp opens in July for the summer.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202606/bg2026060407.webp)

After registration, you'll receive a specific topic. You need to submit a project proposal and outcomes within 72 hours. The committee reviews submissions, followed by presentations/communication sessions. Before the camp starts, outstanding works will be selected and awarded prizes totaling 200,000 RMB.

After the camp opens, over the 2-month summer period, a team of senior mentors and teaching assistants will guide participants in completing a real project.
