# Education Is the Overlooked AI Product Opportunity — and Giants Are Quietly Moving In

## I. A Dinner Conversation

What kind of AI product has the greatest market opportunity?

At a recent dinner with friends, the conversation turned to this question.

One friend said that while everyone is excited about AI coding, AI office tools, AI video, and even AI healthcare, there is one product direction with enormous potential that has been largely overlooked.

AI education.

On one hand, AI is exceptionally good at spreading knowledge — it explains problems clearly and generates text, images, audio, and video that can all be used for teaching. On the other hand, students — and society as a whole — need to update their knowledge, and schools urgently need AI tools. Combine those two sides and ask yourself: how big is the opportunity?

China already has a massive tutoring and supplementary education market, with a scale hard to fathom. Now it is AI's turn to enter.

Yet few companies are building AI education products. Everyone is clustered around coding and office tools. Education barely appears in daily discussion or coverage — isn't that a little strange?

He gave an example: have you heard of [Gauth](https://www.gauthmath.com/)? It is a top education app in the US market, widely used by American students.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091408.webp)

Few people in China know it exists — and even fewer know that **it is a ByteDance product**.

ByteDance also developed a similar education app for the domestic market called "Doubao Study" (豆包爱学), technically related to Gauth, exploring AI-assisted teaching.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091617.webp)

This piqued my curiosity. What can these education products actually do?

## II. Synchronized Curriculum

After that dinner, I kept thinking about what my friend said. I imagined what such a product should look like, and "Doubao Study" became my main reference.

First, the entry point should offer a synchronized curriculum: tutoring content aligned with the official teaching syllabus and textbooks, in sync with classroom instruction.

It should have grade-level entries covering K–12:

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091601.webp)

Within each grade, every subject has its own entry with tutoring content following the textbook order.

For example, a math synchronized curriculum might look like this, letting students find any concept at a glance:

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091401.webp)

## III. AI Video Lessons

Curriculum tutoring shouldn't be pure text and images — video should be fully leveraged.

AI video is surging; it can absolutely be used to generate lessons. A few minutes per segment, with characters, a storyline, vivid visuals, and explanations — far more engaging than dry text. Video lessons can be reused: after mass generation they become a teaching video library with very low ongoing costs.

Doubao Study has a feature called "Doubao Classroom" dedicated to AI video lessons. There are already quite a few video lessons for classical Chinese poetry and math.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091609.webp)

One series, made in collaboration with actor Tang Guoqiang, covers the *Chu Shi Biao* (Memorial on Taking the Field) across three videos, combining visual footage and commentary to help students understand the classical text.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091610.webp)

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091615.webp)

It applies the Seedance model's video generation capability to teaching. The in-video comment streams are very active — I think the approach is correct. Students could eventually watch AI teaching videos the way they watch movies, and even request custom AI-generated content on demand.

## IV. Q&A

Beyond AI video lessons and synchronized curriculum, Doubao Study has a dedicated Q&A entry. After entering learning mode, students can input any need directly.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091618.webp)

**(1) AI Chat**

The simplest use: talk directly with the AI.

Tell the AI what you want to learn (e.g., "Pythagorean theorem") and it gives a brief text explanation plus an interactive web visualization.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091501.webp)

For complex topics, the AI can generate study materials and printable lecture notes for deeper study on a computer.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091603.webp)

It can even generate full courses.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091616.webp)

**(2) Voice Chat**

In addition to text, voice input is supported. With the AI set to respond by voice too, it becomes real-time voice conversation — almost like talking to a human teacher.

You can schedule regular sessions. The AI asks questions based on your study content, giving quizzes and reviews — for example, guiding you through reciting a text passage. When you need a break, it can tell a story or a joke.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091605.webp)

Another important application: English speaking practice. The AI provides a scenario and asks you to respond or repeat in English.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091606.webp)

It corrects pronunciation, vocabulary, and grammar in real time, and can adjust speaking pace.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091604.webp)

**(3) Photo Upload**

You can also photograph exercises, homework, or exam papers and upload the images.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091405.webp)

For multi-page exams, you can photograph and upload multiple pages at once for unified processing.

The AI explains the reasoning and step-by-step derivation for each question.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202609/bg2026091607.webp)

If your handwritten answers are in the photo, the AI automatically reads them and marks them correct or incorrect.

## V. Looking Ahead

This is what I envision an AI education app to look like — and Doubao Study shows what that implementation can be.

Is there a market opportunity here?

Under the pressure of AI, both classroom education and the tutoring industry face major transformation. Whoever best solves AI-assisted teaching has the greatest opportunity — and the technology is already capable of delivering it.

An AI education product that uses AI video and audio to simulate human-teacher interaction, combined with text and visual Q&A, photo-based homework help, and automated grading, has enormous market demand. Schools, parents, and students would all likely embrace it.

Traditional learning devices costing thousands of yuan sold well in their day. An AI product with far greater capabilities and abundant free content can definitely find broad adoption.

Doubao Study is a pioneer in this space — moving while others are still overlooking the opportunity. Its biggest advantage is the in-house Seedance 2.5 video model, giving it an edge in AI video content. I'll watch it as a bellwether, tracking future developments and innovations to understand how to seize the AI education opportunity.
