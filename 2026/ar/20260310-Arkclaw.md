## III. Cloud Lobster Farming

ArkClaw is a "cloud-hosted OpenClaw" — OpenClaw installed on Volcengine (ByteDance's AI cloud service) cloud hosts. The "Ark" in its name refers to "Ark" (as in Volcengine Ark).

In addition to "cloud-hosted OpenClaw," you can also install OpenClaw on a local computer.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202603/bg2026031008.webp)

For those unfamiliar, you might wonder about the difference. Let me briefly explain.

First, you need to understand that **OpenClaw is an automation software** — users describe their needs in natural language, and it uses large models to figure out how to meet those needs, then automatically completes the tasks.

When installed on a local computer (your laptop), it can automatically operate local files and devices — for example, "find photos taken on this day last year" or "turn off the living room smart light and check the last week's power consumption."

When installed on the cloud, it can interact 24/7 with various web services — for example, "when an email is received, automatically generate a 30-character summary and send a notification to your phone."

So if you need automated web service operations that require long-term online access or daily scheduled tasks, cloud-hosted OpenClaw is the right choice.

After retrieving information, OpenClaw can send it to your phone.

The other two require more complex setup; see the [official documentation](https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6396/2249541?lang=zh) for details.

Click the "Feishu Pairing" button above the dialog box. (The "Message Channel" button is for binding WeCom and DingTalk.)

## V. Skills and Other Settings

### 5.1 Skills

OpenClaw's own capabilities are limited — it will inevitably encounter problems it doesn't know how to handle. That's when Skills come in to extend its capabilities, greatly increasing OpenClaw's usefulness.

What is a Skill? Simply put, it's a file package containing instructions and examples that teach the model how to complete specific tasks.

Many pre-written Skills are available online; just pick the ones you need and load them into OpenClaw. The website [ClawHub.ai](https://clawhub.ai/) collects OpenClaw-specific Skills, with nearly 20,000 available.

![](https://cdn.beekka.com/blogimg/asset/202603/bg2026031113.webp)

OpenClaw didn't know how to use Amap before, but with this skill, it learns and can query information from it. You can view the skill's details on [its homepage](https://clawhub.ai/Dboy233/amap).
